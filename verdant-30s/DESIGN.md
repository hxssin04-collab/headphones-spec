# Verdant — compact emerald headset

The user-approved emerald/teal LED style is retained. The new design shortens and broadens the earcups, retains the circular headband, and uses white leather cushions, brushed silver housings, compact pivots and a small tapered microphone capsule.

## Preview construction

A continuous 30-second, 24 fps camera move is rendered from the generated source still. Smooth displacement animates material patterns within carefully drawn masks; edge attenuation protects the hardware. Light variation and subtle local spill are coordinated. The camera gently pushes in and drifts sideways. There is no repeated short segment or synthesized view of hidden surfaces.

The browser GIF covers the same full 30 seconds at reduced resolution. The downloadable H.264 MP4 has 720 frames at 1152 × 768.

## Status

Visual concept, not footage of physical hardware. Actual mass, acoustics, battery life, thermal behavior, fit, comfort and manufacturability require engineering validation.
