# Vanguard: refined gaming headset concept

Preserves the accepted tall silhouette, roomy white leather cushions, compact rear-mounted cup attachments and violet/blue/crimson illuminated camo. The microphone now uses a flattened metal boom, a short flexible root and a compact metal capsule. Dark metal shoulders and small controls strengthen the gaming character without adding wraparound fork arms.

## Animation

A continuous 15-second shot at 24 fps. The source artwork is a generated still. Local material displacement and coordinated light variation are masked to the optical shells; a gentle camera push and lateral drift run across the complete shot. No repeated five-second segment, hidden-angle reconstruction, or claim of a physical working sample.

## Engineering status

Illustrative design only. Compact construction is intended to reduce visual bulk. Actual mass, balance, clamp force, battery life, heat, acoustic performance, hinge durability and long-session comfort require engineering and prototype validation.
